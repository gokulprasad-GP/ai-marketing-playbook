## Why this exists
AI is changing marketing faster than most marketing organizations can adapt. Teams experiment with tools in pockets, but few have a **clear point of view on where AI creates real business value**, how to govern it, and how to change the way marketing teams work.

This playbook is my attempt to close that gap. It brings together the frameworks, prompts and operating principles I use to think about **AI as a growth capability, not just a productivity tool**.

> *AI won't replace marketers. Marketers who know how to lead with AI will replace those who don't.*

## The core idea: four layers of AI value

| Layer | Question it answers | Where AI helps |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Insight** | What is really happening with our customers and market? | Research synthesis, social listening, review mining, segmentation |
| **2. Strategy** | Where should we play and how do we win? | Scenario planning, audience strategy, budget allocation, brief writing |
| **3. Execution** | How do we create and deliver at speed and scale? | Creative variation, copy, localization, media setup, QA |
| **4. Learning** | What worked, why, and what do we do next? | Measurement, MMM interpretation, test design, insight storytelling |

Most teams start at **Execution**. The biggest long-term value sits in **Insight** and **Learning**, where better decisions compound over time.

## How to use the prompts
1. **Give context first.** Every prompt works better when you share your category, audience, objective and constraints.
2. **Treat outputs as a first draft.** AI accelerates thinking; it does not replace judgment.
3. **Never paste confidential data** into tools that are not approved by your organization.
4. **Iterate.** Ask the model to critique its own answer, argue the opposite view, or rewrite for a different audience.

Prompts use `[BRACKETS]` for the details you fill in.

## Principles I lead with
- **Customer first:** AI should help us understand people better, not just reach them more cheaply.
- **Brand and performance are one system:** use AI to strengthen both, not to trade one for the other.
- **Human judgment stays in the loop:** for strategy, brand voice, ethics and anything customer-facing.
- **Build capability, not dependency:** train teams, document playbooks, and own the learning in-house.
- **Measure what matters:** connect AI investments to business outcomes, not tool usage.
