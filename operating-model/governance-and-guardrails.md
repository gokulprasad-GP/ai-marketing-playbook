# 🏛️ Governance & Guardrails

AI at scale needs trust. Clear guardrails let teams move faster, not slower, because everyone knows what's safe.

## Responsible AI principles for marketing
1. **Privacy first:** never enter personal, confidential or customer data into unapproved tools.
2. **Human accountability:** a named person approves anything customer-facing.
3. **Brand integrity:** AI output must meet the same brand, legal and quality standards as human work.
4. **Transparency:** follow platform and regulatory rules on disclosing AI-generated or synthetic content.
5. **Fairness:** check targeting, imagery and language for bias or exclusion.
6. **IP respect:** don't imitate real people, protected characters or other brands' work.

## Traffic-light framework for use cases

| 🟢 Green: go | 🟡 Amber: review | 🔴 Red: not allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Brainstorming and ideation | Customer-facing copy and visuals | Uploading customer personal data to unapproved tools |
| Summarizing public research | Synthetic voices or people in ads | Impersonating real people or brands |
| Drafting internal documents | Automated optimization rules | Making claims that can't be substantiated |
| Learning and training | Use of first-party data in AI tools | Bypassing legal or brand approval |

## Team operating model

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Executive sponsor** | Sets the vision, priorities and investment for AI in marketing |
| **AI council** (marketing, legal, IT, data, privacy) | Approves tools, sets policy, reviews risk |
| **AI champions** (one per team) | Find use cases, coach peers, share learnings |
| **Every marketer** | Builds AI fluency and applies the guardrails day to day |

## Rhythm
- **Monthly:** champions share wins, failures and new use cases.
- **Quarterly:** council reviews tools, policy and the use-case pipeline.
- **Annually:** refresh the maturity assessment and set the next-stage priorities.

## Measuring success
Track AI's impact on **speed** (time to brief, launch and insight), **quality** (creative effectiveness, error rates), **capability** (team fluency and adoption), and ultimately **business outcomes**.
