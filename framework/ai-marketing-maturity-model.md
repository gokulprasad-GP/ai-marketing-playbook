# 📈 AI Marketing Maturity Model

A simple way for marketing leaders to assess where their organization is today and what it takes to move forward.

| Stage | Name | What it looks like | What leaders should focus on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Curious** | Individuals try AI tools on their own. No shared guidance or approved tools. | Set basic guardrails, approve a safe toolset, and encourage learning. |
| **2** | **Experimenting** | Teams run pilots in pockets (copy, images, summaries). Wins are anecdotal. | Pick 3–5 high-value use cases, define success measures, and share learnings openly. |
| **3** | **Operationalizing** | Proven use cases become standard workflows with templates, training and owners. | Build playbooks, upskill the team, and integrate AI into briefs, planning and QA. |
| **4** | **Scaling** | AI is embedded across insight, creative, media and measurement. Data foundations are connected. | Invest in first-party data, measurement, governance and cross-functional alignment. |
| **5** | **AI-Native** | Marketing is designed around human + AI collaboration. Decisions are faster, smarter and continuously learning. | Redesign roles and operating models; use AI as a strategic advantage, not just efficiency. |

## Self-assessment questions
Score each from 1 (not at all) to 5 (fully in place):

**Strategy & leadership**
- Do we have a clear point of view on where AI creates value for our marketing?
- Are AI priorities linked to business outcomes, not just efficiency?

**People & skills**
- Does every marketer have basic AI fluency?
- Do we have champions who coach others and share best practices?

**Process & workflow**
- Are proven AI use cases documented as repeatable workflows?
- Is AI built into briefs, planning, creative development and QA?

**Data & measurement**
- Is our first-party data organized, consented and usable?
- Can we connect marketing activity to business results (e.g., MMM, experiments)?

**Governance & trust**
- Do we have clear rules on data privacy, brand safety, IP and disclosure?
- Is there human review for anything customer-facing?

**Reading your score:** an average under 2 means you are at Stage 1–2; 2–3.5 is Stage 3; above 3.5 is Stage 4–5.

## The leadership shift
Moving up the curve is less about tools and more about **leadership choices**:
- From *"Which tool should we buy?"* → to *"Which decisions should get better?"*
- From *individual productivity* → to *team and enterprise capability*
- From *pilots* → to *operating model change*
