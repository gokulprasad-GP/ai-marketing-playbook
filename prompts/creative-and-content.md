# 🎨 Creative & Content Prompts

AI is a powerful creative partner for exploring ideas quickly. Final creative judgment, brand voice and legal review remain human responsibilities.

---

### 1. Brief builder
```
Help me write a creative brief for [CAMPAIGN/PRODUCT].
Ask me up to 8 clarifying questions first. Then produce a brief with: business
objective, communication objective, target audience insight, single-minded
proposition, reasons to believe, tone, mandatories, and how we'll measure success.
```

### 2. Insight to idea
```
Customer insight: [INSIGHT].
Generate 5 distinct creative territories that bring this insight to life. For each,
give a name, the core idea in one sentence, an example headline, and how it could
work across video, social and out-of-home. Make them genuinely different from each other.
```

### 3. Variation at scale
```
Here is our hero message: [MESSAGE].
Create 10 variations for [CHANNEL] tailored to these audiences: [AUDIENCES].
Keep the brand voice [DESCRIBE VOICE]. Respect these limits: [CHARACTER LIMITS].
Label each with the audience and the hypothesis it tests.
```

### 4. Brand voice check
```
Here are our brand voice guidelines: [GUIDELINES].
Review the copy below and score it 1–5 on each guideline. Highlight anything
off-brand and suggest a rewrite that stays true to the voice. [COPY]
```

### 5. Cultural & sensitivity review
```
Review this campaign concept for [MARKET/AUDIENCE]. Flag anything that could be
misunderstood, insensitive or controversial, and explain why. Suggest alternatives.
Note: this is a first-pass check and does not replace local expert review. [CONCEPT]
```

### 6. Content repurposing
```
Turn this [WEBINAR / ARTICLE / VIDEO TRANSCRIPT] into: one LinkedIn post, three
short social captions, one email newsletter section and five headline options.
Keep the core message consistent and adapt the tone for each format. [CONTENT]
```
