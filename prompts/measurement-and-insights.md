# 📊 Measurement & Insight Prompts

The most underrated use of AI in marketing: turning complex data into clear decisions and stories leaders can act on.

---

### 1. MMM results explainer
```
Below are the key outputs from our media mix model: [PASTE CONTRIBUTION, ROI,
RESPONSE CURVES OR SUMMARY]. Explain the results in plain language for an executive
audience. Cover: what is driving the business, which channels are saturated or
under-invested, what we should change, and the limitations of the model.
```

### 2. Insight storytelling
```
Here is a set of findings: [DATA / NOTES].
Turn them into a story using this structure: the situation, the surprise, why it
matters, and what we recommend. Keep it to five short paragraphs and suggest one
chart that would make the point clearly.
```

### 3. KPI framework design
```
Our business objective is [OBJECTIVE]. Design a KPI framework that connects
marketing activity to business outcomes, with three levels: business outcomes,
marketing outcomes, and leading indicators. For each KPI, explain why it matters
and how often it should be reviewed.
```

### 4. Learning agenda
```
Based on our goals for [PERIOD] and the uncertainties below, build a learning agenda:
the 5–7 most important questions we need to answer, the method for each (experiment,
MMM, research, analytics), and the timing. Uncertainties: [LIST]
```

### 5. SQL helper (for GA4 / BigQuery users)
```
Write a BigQuery SQL query against the GA4 export dataset [PROJECT.DATASET] that
[DESCRIBE THE QUESTION, e.g., counts users by traffic source who completed a key
event within 7 days of first visit]. Explain each step in comments and note any
assumptions I should check.
```

### 6. Board-ready summary
```
Summarize marketing performance for [PERIOD] in a format suitable for the board.
Lead with business impact, then the three biggest wins, two risks, and what we are
doing next. Avoid jargon and keep it under 200 words. [PASTE RESULTS]
```
