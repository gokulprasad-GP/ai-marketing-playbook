# 🎯 Media & Performance Prompts

Use AI to plan smarter, test more rigorously and catch issues before they cost money. Always validate recommendations against your own data and platform reality.

---

### 1. Channel role mapping
```
We are planning [CAMPAIGN] with the objective of [OBJECTIVE] for [AUDIENCE].
For each channel in [CHANNEL LIST], define its role in the funnel (reach, consideration,
conversion, retention), the right success metric, and how it should work with the
other channels. Highlight where channels may overlap or compete.
```

### 2. Test & learn design
```
I want to test [HYPOTHESIS] in [CHANNEL/MARKET].
Design an experiment: test vs. control setup, key metric, minimum duration,
what could bias the result, and how we will decide what to do next.
Explain it simply enough for a non-technical stakeholder.
```

### 3. Campaign QA checklist
```
Create a pre-launch QA checklist for a [CHANNEL] campaign covering: targeting,
budgets and pacing, creative specs, tracking and tags, landing pages, naming
conventions, brand safety, and legal/compliance. Format as a checklist I can reuse.
```

### 4. Performance diagnosis
```
Here is a summary of campaign performance: [PASTE TABLE OR SUMMARY].
Act as a performance marketing lead. Identify what's working, what isn't, the most
likely reasons, and the top five actions to take this week, ranked by expected impact
and effort. Call out anything that looks like a tracking issue rather than a real trend.
```

### 5. Agency brief & scorecard
```
Help me design an agency scorecard for [SCOPE OF SERVICES]. Include categories for
strategic thinking, execution quality, responsiveness, innovation and business
impact. Suggest how to weight each and how often to review.
```

### 6. Budget scenario thinking
```
We have [BUDGET RANGE] for [PERIOD]. Propose three allocation scenarios across
[CHANNELS]: one reach-led, one efficiency-led, and one balanced. Explain the
trade-offs of each and what we would expect to learn.
```
